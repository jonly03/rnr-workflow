Questions

Auto glass FAQ

How much does windshield replacement cost in Boston?

It depends on your vehicle, the glass, and features like rain sensors and driver-assistance cameras. The fastest way to an exact price is to start a case online or text a photo of the damage and your VIN to 857-266-1284 — we quote the real number up front, with no obligation.

Does insurance cover windshield replacement in Massachusetts?

Usually, yes. Glass damage is typically covered under the comprehensive portion of your policy, and many Massachusetts policies carry a $0 glass deductible. We work with Progressive, Geico, Mapfre, Farmers, Quincy Mutual and other insurers, and handle the claim paperwork for you.

Do you offer mobile windshield replacement?

Yes — mobile service is what we do. A technician comes to your home, office, or job site anywhere in Greater Boston. Prefer the shop? We are at 118 Terrace St, Boston, MA 02120.

How long does a replacement take, and when can I drive?

Most installs take about an hour. The urethane then needs time to cure before the car is safe to drive — your technician gives you the exact safe drive-away time for your job before you leave.

Can a chip be repaired instead of replacing the windshield?

Often, yes. Small chips and short cracks outside the driver's direct line of sight can usually be repaired in about 30 minutes for less than a replacement — and repairs are often fully covered by insurance. Send us a photo and we will tell you straight which one you need.

Will my safety cameras work after the new windshield?

Many newer vehicles have driver-assistance (ADAS) cameras behind the windshield that need recalibration after a replacement. We tell you before the job whether your vehicle needs it and what it costs — no surprises.

Do you work with body shops, auctions, and fleets?

Yes. We do removal & reinstallation and replacement work for body shops, auctions, dealers, and fleets across Greater Boston, with fast turnaround so vehicles are not sitting waiting on glass. Call 857-266-1284 to set up partner work.
