Phase 1 - Week 1: prove the funnel works
A customer who finds you and then hits a wall is worse than a customer who never found you - they leave with
a story. On 13 November the intake failed twice in one night: a Vercel login wall until about 23:30 EST, then a
database that had auto-paused after 7 days idle. You resumed it that night, and health was confirmed healthy at
23:47 EST - health returns ok, storage Postgres. Phase 1 makes that recovery permanent and proves it.

Step 1.1 - End-to-end test case (B, then P to confirm receipt)
☐ Open the public intake in a private browser window, signed out, exactly as a customer would.
☐ Submit one clearly labelled test case through all 3 steps - vehicle, glass, contact.
☐ Confirm the case appears in the case dashboard. A form that loads is not proof; a case record is.
☐ Confirm the health check returns healthy before and after the test.
☐ Delete or close the test case so it never mixes with real work.

Step 1.2 - Stop the 7-day pause for good (P decision, B sets it up)
Free Supabase projects pause after 7 days without activity, and a project left paused for 90 days can be
deleted. Staging will go quiet again - that is normal for a test system, and fatal for a customer funnel.

   Short term (free): a scheduled weekly keep-alive ping to the health endpoint, so the database never
   reaches 7 idle days. Babe can set this up and report any failed ping the same day.
   Right term: the customer intake should run on a production project that does not pause - either a paid tier
   for the production database, or a production project kept active by real traffic plus the same keep-alive. Price
   and choice are on the decision sheet, page 12.
   Rule from now on: the word "staging" never appears in a public customer URL. Until a production intake
   address exists, every "Start a Case" button keeps pointing to the current intake - and its health is checked
   weekly.

    Do not skip the weekly check. The 13 November failure was silent. No customer complained; the funnel
    simply could not accept a case. A weekly health check with a same-day alert is the difference between a
    10-minute fix and a lost week of customers.
